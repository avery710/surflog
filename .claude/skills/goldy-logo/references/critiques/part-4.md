# Anonymous logo critiques, part 4

Each case is one logo submitted for review: the submitter's brief (if any), then numbered critique notes, each pinned to a region of the image (coarse zone plus approximate position from the top-left). Names are replaced with [name]. Images are not included. The original text is Russian and often profane; this translation keeps the standards and the bluntness, never the profanity.
